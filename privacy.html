Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Overview

Pinterest Viewer is a personal, private tool used solely by its developer to access their own Pinterest boards and saved pins. It is not a public service and does not collect, store, or share any data from other users.

Data Collected

The application accesses Pinterest board and pin data through the official Pinterest API, using OAuth 2.0 authentication. No data is stored on external servers. Access tokens are stored locally on the developer's own machine.

Third-Party Services

This application uses the Pinterest API. Pinterest's own Privacy Policy applies to all data accessed through their platform.
